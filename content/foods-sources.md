# Food table sources (content/foods.ts)

## Licences
- USDA FoodData Central, SR Legacy (April 2018 release, bulk CSV): public domain (CC0 / US Government work). Cite: U.S. Department of Agriculture, Agricultural Research Service, FoodData Central, fdc.nal.usda.gov.
- Open Food Facts (OFF): Open Database Licence (ODbL) v1.0, database contents licence DbCL. Attribution: Open Food Facts contributors, openfoodfacts.org. Values are crowd-sourced from product labels; we used only Latvian-market products whose macros pass the Atwater check.
- Composites are our own arithmetic on the USDA values above; recipe weights are stated per entry and are estimates of a typical home or restaurant portion, not measurements.

## Method
- USDA values read from the SR Legacy CSV bulk download (nutrient ids 1008 energy kcal, 1003 protein, 1005 carbohydrate, 1004 fat). No numbers taken from memory.
- OFF values read from the Open Food Facts search API (search.openfoodfacts.org) per barcode, countries filter Latvia, rounded as printed on the label.
- Liquids: USDA gives per 100 g. Converted to per 100 ml using a stated density (milk 1.03, juice and cola 1.04, beer 1.01, wine 0.99, vodka 0.95, others 1.0). Portion "grams" for liquid foods are millilitres. OFF liquids are used as printed (EU labels state per 100 ml).
- Composite per-100 g = sum of component nutrients / stated cooked total weight x 100. Garri, fufu and eba totals are derived from the dry matter of raw cassava (FDC 169985, water content from nutrient 1051): garri 10 percent moisture, fufu 65 percent, eba 62 percent.
- Atwater check: kcal compared with 4 x protein + 4 x carbs + 9 x fat, tolerance max(12 percent, 5 kcal). Alcohol drinks are excepted (ethanol 7 kcal/g).
- Rounding: kcal to whole numbers, macros to 0.1 g.
- Household portion weights (egg 50 g, slice 30 g, cup 240 ml, etc.) are typical values, not USDA nutrient data; they only scale the verified per-100 values.

The kcal figure is the correct one to use.
